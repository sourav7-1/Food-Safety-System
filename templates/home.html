Seamless Process

From registration to resolution in three steps.

  1. 1

    Register Vendor

    Submit stall details, operating license, and food categories for administrative verification.

  2. 2

    Conduct & Score Audit

    Inspectors assess food prep and hygiene on-site; the risk engine computes a live safety grade.

  3. 3

    Track & Resolve Alerts

    Vendors submit corrective proof; officials track resolution and public confidence stays high.

Why It Matters

Campus health, backed by transparent data.

Healthy campus dining requires consistent, transparent standards. DIU Food Safety System gives vendors clear guidance toward total hygiene compliance, gives health officers real-time operational data, and empowers students to eat with confidence.

By centralizing inspection histories and automating risk classifications, we eliminate guesswork and maintain the highest health standards across every campus food outlet.

Total Transparency Open, verifiable hygiene grades for every campus dining spot.
Safer Dining Early detection of safety risks before health issues arise.
