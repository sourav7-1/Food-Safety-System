Why It Matters

Public health, backed by data

Street food is a vital part of city life — and keeping it safe requires more than paper checklists. Street Food Safety brings transparency to the inspection process, gives vendors a clear path to compliance, and gives officials the data they need to act on risk before it becomes a public health incident.

By centralizing inspection history and automating risk scoring, the platform helps cities enforce standards consistently and fairly, while building public trust in the food vendors that communities rely on every day.

Transparency Open, consistent inspection records for every stall.
Safer Food Earlier detection of risk before it reaches the public.
Data-Driven Enforcement Risk scores that prioritize where inspectors act next.
